# Channel Playbooks

## Playbook A — Direct DM for niche builders

### When to use
- the ICP is specific
- the founder knows the community vocabulary
- the problem is concrete and urgent

### Opening line pattern
- mention a real trigger
- mention one relevant pain
- ask one small question

### CTA pattern
- one next step only
- low friction
- no multi-choice confusion

### Good example

> Hey — saw you working on [specific thing]. I noticed a common issue around [pain] when teams hit [trigger]. Curious whether that is showing up for you too?

### Bad example

> Hey, I built an amazing all-in-one platform that can transform your workflow. Want to hop on a call?

## Playbook B — Community value-first

### When to use
- the ICP hangs out in public communities
- trust is built by helping first
- the founder can answer specific questions

### Good behavior
- answer the question directly
- add context the community does not already know
- avoid moving to pitch too early

### Bad behavior
- generic self-promotion
- repeated link dumping
- forcing an offer into every thread

## Playbook C — LinkedIn role-based

### When to use
- the buyer is a job title, not a hobbyist identity
- the pain belongs to a function or workflow

### Good example

> Noticed teams in your role often get stuck when [trigger]. I’m researching how people currently solve that — mind if I ask one quick question?

### Bad example

> We help businesses scale faster with AI.

## Playbook D — X / Twitter insight-led

### When to use
- the ICP likes ideas and public progress
- the audience responds to sharp observations

### Good pattern
- one observation
- one contrarian insight
- one proof point
- one invitation to DM

### Bad pattern
- vague motivation posting
- endless build-in-public without buyer signal
- engagement bait with no market relevance

## Playbook E — Marketplace listing

### When to use
- there is already search intent
- the offer can be summarized in one sentence
- trust can be enhanced by the platform

### Good listing traits
- category clarity
- strong headline
- proof signal
- pricing transparency

### Bad listing traits
- clever but unclear copy
- category mismatch
- too many features in the title

## v1.6 example expansion

### Direct outbound example
- Target subcommunity: [specific niche]
- Trigger moment: [what happened recently]
- Hook: [problem name]
- CTA: [one action only]
- Success benchmark: [response / conversation / ask]

### Owned audience example
- Target subcommunity: existing subscribers or followers
- Hook: teach one new insight
- CTA: reply with their current workaround
- Success benchmark: conversation rate and conversion to ask

### Partner example
- Partner type: [agency / creator / tool]
- Trust transfer: [why partner credibility matters]
- CTA: co-branded audit / referral / integration
- Success benchmark: warm intro quality
