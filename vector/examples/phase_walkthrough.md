# Phase Walkthrough

## Scenario 1 — Builder with an early product and no traction

### Intake
The builder has a product and a rough idea of the target user, but no verified distribution motion yet.

### ICP/JTBD
The system should sharpen the problem from “people who need this” to a specific trigger moment and buyer identity.

### Market terrain
Map competitors and substitutes before choosing channels.

### Channel scoring
Shortlist channels based on where the user actually exists.

### Thesis
Pick one primary channel and define the initial offer shape.

### Venue
Choose the smallest trustworthy selling surface.

### Signal loop
Track whether the response quality validates the ICP.

## Scenario 2 — Existing product, weak signal

### What usually fails
- founder keeps changing tactics
- no benchmark exists
- KPIs are vague
- every channel is treated the same

### What VECTOR does
- restores the latest state
- checks drift
- re-scores the channel set
- decides whether to continue, adjust, or pivot

## Scenario 3 — Strong audience, unclear product fit

### What usually fails
A large audience does not mean a buyer audience.

### What VECTOR does
- separates audience from buyer intent
- checks who is already asking for what
- looks for organic asks, shares, or direct inbound
