# Bad Outputs

## Bad example 1: everything for everyone

**Product:** “AI GTM OS for everyone”  
**Price:** undefined  
**Audience:** founders, marketers, agencies, students, operators  
**Problem:** unspecified

Why it is bad:

- the ICP is the entire market
- the message cannot be tested
- the offer has no clear starting point
- the package is impossible to route

## Bad example 2: too many channels at once

“We should do SEO, cold email, LinkedIn, X, Reddit, partnerships, and paid ads at the same time.”

Why it is bad:

- no focus
- no evidence hierarchy
- no way to know what worked
- it hides a weak thesis behind activity

## Bad example 3: venue without trust

“Let’s sell a high-ticket offer through one generic landing page.”

Why it is bad:

- no trust signal
- no clear entry offer
- no buyer behavior match
- no reason to believe the conversion path

## Bad example 4: signal without learning

- 0 attempts
- 0 responses
- decision: scale paid ads anyway

Why it is bad:

- no data
- no benchmark
- no learning loop
- the next step is a guess, not a decision

## Concrete bad example

**Product:** AI GTM OS  
**Audience:** everyone with a business  
**Channel:** all of them  
**Venue:** one homepage  
**Result target:** “more revenue”

Why it is bad:
- the package is un-routable
- the buyer cannot see themselves in it
- the system cannot tell whether it is working
- the output hides uncertainty instead of managing it
