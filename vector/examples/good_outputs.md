## Good example 1: early-stage B2B tool

**Product:** VectorCRM Lite  
**ICP:** solo technical founders who already have users but no repeatable distribution  
**Live status:** live  
**Goal:** choose one channel, one venue, and one believable first offer

### Thesis card

- Primary channel: direct DM
- Why this channel: fastest path to a real conversation
- Growth multiplier: second channel after the first 3 conversations prove the offer
- Unlock condition: 3 qualified replies from the same ICP
- Reversibility: if reply quality is weak, narrow the audience before changing the offer
- Evidence used: existing community presence, prior replies, and observed pain
- Alternatives rejected: SEO and broad content because the feedback loop is too slow

### Venue card

- Sales venue: async teardown
- Entry offer: short audit
- Core offer: implementation sprint
- Upsell: monthly advisory
- Trust signal needed: one before/after example and one short testimonial
- Venue risk: buyer wants proof before talking
- ICP drift check: if replies come from the wrong company size, tighten the audience

### Signal example

- 28 genuine attempts
- 4 replies
- 2 meaningful conversations
- 1 clear ask
- decision: keep the channel, improve the message, and write the results into the ledger

## Good example 2: marketplace-led motion

**Product:** OpsKit for Notion  
**ICP:** teams already searching for a plug-and-play operations toolkit  
**Channel:** marketplace listing  
**Venue:** direct checkout with a low-friction entry package  
**Trust signal:** screenshots, templates, and clear customer outcomes  
**Why it is good:** the buyer already has intent, the venue matches that intent, and the offer is simple enough to test quickly.

## Why these are good

- the ICP is specific
- the channel is testable
- the venue matches the trust level
- the thesis says what would break it
- the outputs are decision-ready, not vague
